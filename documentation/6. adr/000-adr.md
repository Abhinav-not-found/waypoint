## ADR - Architecture decision records

An ADR documents why you made a particular technical decision, not just what you implemented

Example:

```
documentation/
└── ard/
    ├── 0000-ard.md
    ├── 0001-use-postgresql.md
    ├── 0002-use-redis.md
    ├── 0003-monorepo-architecture.md
    └── 0004-use-rest-api.md
```
